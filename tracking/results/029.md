## Decisions

- permission scope metadata is organization-only or project-safe; stable keys cannot disappear during sync; system role metadata and grants are ordinary-application read-only while the advisory-locked catalog sync is the sole controlled reconciliation path; authorization remains permission-based and never role-name-based

## Remaining risks

- A privileged direct database operator can bypass model immutability; revision checksums and deployment review are the detection/governance boundary. A reviewed future YAML revision may deliberately remove a system-role grant
- so grant reductions require explicit security review. Role assignments and effective permission decisions are intentionally deferred to subsequent prompts.

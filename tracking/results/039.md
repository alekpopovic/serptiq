## Migrations

## Decisions

- Meter definitions and rates are immutable and versioned with exact catalog synchronization
- Provider-period meters require an explicit billing period and active immutable subscription context while calendar meters use UTC month boundaries
- Corrections append opposite-signed events tied to the original context and cannot cumulatively overcompensate
- Pool summaries aggregate compatible meter windows sharing the same pool and period and subscription snapshot
- Raw idempotency keys and provider references are retained only as SHA-256 digests
